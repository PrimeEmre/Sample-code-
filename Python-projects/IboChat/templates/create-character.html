Create Your Own Character

Make up a name and personality — no real person involved. Optionally record your own voice (or someone else's, with their consent) for the character to speak with.

Read this out loud, clearly, in a quiet room — the longer and cleaner this recording is, the less robotic the clone will sound (aim for at least 15-20 seconds):

"Hi there, this is my own voice, and I'm excited to bring my character to life. I like walking outside on a sunny afternoon, grabbing a coffee with friends, and talking about whatever comes to mind. It's funny how a normal Tuesday can turn into one of the best days of the week if you're around the right people."
